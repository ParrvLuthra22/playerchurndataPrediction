1) Personalized Win-Back Offer
For players flagged as high churn risk, deliver a limited-time in-game reward matched to their favorite genre or play history.

2) Session Re-Activation Campaign
If weekly sessions drop significantly, trigger a targeted notification with one-click resume and a short challenge objective.

3) Dynamic Difficulty and Onboarding Support
Players with low progression should receive adaptive difficulty tuning, contextual tips, and optional guided missions.

4) Social Retention Hooks
Encourage players to join squads, friend groups, or guilds; social ties increase return probability and session consistency.

5) Event-Based Engagement Boost
Run weekend micro-events with clear goals and visible rewards for at-risk players to rebuild routine play behavior.

6) Loyalty Milestones
Introduce streak rewards and milestone bonuses tied to consecutive login days or completed missions.

7) Personalized Content Recommendations
Recommend game modes and missions based on prior session duration and achievement patterns to reduce decision fatigue.

8) Inactivity Escalation Workflow
When inactivity crosses thresholds (e.g., 3, 7, 14 days), escalate from reminder to incentive to premium offer.

9) Early Friction Detection
Detect repeated short sessions or repeated losses and proactively surface support, easier missions, or starter packs.

10) Follow-Up Measurement Loop
After each retention action, track post-intervention sessions and churn probability changes to optimize future campaigns.
